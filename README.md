Phishing Awareness Guide
1. INTRODUCTION
   Phishing is a cybercrime technique in which attackers use fraudulent emails, text messages or websites to trick people into revealing confidential information such as passwords and banking details.
 2. COMMON WARNING SIGNS
      - Unexpected emails or messages
      - Suspicious links and attachments
      - Requests for passwords or personal information
      - Spelling mistakes or unusual sender addresses
      - Messages creating urgency or threatening account closure
3. TYPES OF PHISHING
   - Email phishing: Fraudulent emails designed to steal personal information
   - Smishing: Phishing attacks carried out through SMS messages
   - Spear phishing: Targeted phishing attacks directed at specific individuals or organisations.
 4. PREVENTION METHODS
    - Avoid clicking suspicious links
    - Verify the identity of unknown senders
    - Use strong and unique passwords
    - Enable multi-factor authentication
    - Keep devices and softaware updated
  5. WHAT RO DO AFTER A PHISHING ATTACK
     - Report the suspicious message to the relevant organisation
     - Change passwords if account details may have been exposed
     - Contact your bank if financial information was compromised
     - Scan affected devices for malware
  6. PROJECT OBJECTIVE
     This project is intended to help students, employees and general internet users recognise phishing attacks and adopt safer online practices.
